# RWX Strategy Guide (Overview)

> RWX (Rusted Warfare, a community version of the game) is an RTS with its own unit counters and tactical schools; knowing them in advance helps you win.
> This guide offers simple guidance and suggestions only. It is **not a guaranteed winning strategy**, and you are free to play your own way.
>
> 中文版：[strategy_zh.md](strategy_zh.md)

**How to read**: this file is the overview and quick-reference entry point. Full coverage of each topic lives in the files under `strategy_detail/`. Content tagged **[Supplement]** is general advice organized and derived from the original experience notes; it **adds no new game numbers**.

> Unit and building names in the English version are translated from the Chinese original. The glossary in [11](strategy_detail/11_counters_glossary.md) maps every name so corrections can be made in one place.

## 1. Ten Core Principles

1. **Do not let most resource points fall into enemy hands**. A resource-point deficit is the hardest to recover from.
2. **Open with builders, then upgrade extractors and build turrets**. With no army, those are the only two things you can do.
3. **Check economy multiplier and income before choosing units**: x1 is poor, x1.5 is barely OK, x2 and above is a normal, smooth game.
4. **Spend your money promptly**. Sitting on a large balance has little benefit unless you are saving for a high-tier unit or building.
5. **Turrets are a very cost-effective defense**: artillery units plus turrets at home push the defensive ceiling close to the offensive ceiling of mobile units.
6. **Never skimp on anti-air**: use T2 anti-air turrets for position defense; T1 gives only weak defense.
7. **In a rush, kill what can fight back first**. Ground armies can ignore anti-air turrets and air forces can ignore ground turrets when time is short.
8. **Mind movement speed differences**: on long attacks, fast units arrive first and take extra losses.
9. **Mechs fear shock and artillery; air fears interceptors and anti-air**. Counters decide cost-efficiency.
10. **The situation flips fast**: even 10 seconds of idling can overturn a game.

## 3. Economy Tiers at a Glance

Income figures are the **in-UI native rate** (per 40 ticks). Per game second = native × 1.5625.

| Tier | Per 40 ticks | One-line advice |
|---|---|---|
| Poor | < 50 | Level-1 tanks, a few Heavy/Plasma Tanks; basic mechs + AA mechs + artillery mechs; Interceptors + Gunships; Battleships |
| Normal start | 50–100 | Units priced up to about 2000 are fine |
| Comfortable | 100–200 | Strong attacks possible; nearly everything except a few very expensive units |
| Rich | 200+ | Nukes can be considered |

Full details: [02 Economy & Expansion](strategy_detail/02_economy.md).
