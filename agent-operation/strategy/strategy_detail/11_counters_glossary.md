## 1. Counter Cheat Sheet

| Unit / type | Countered by | Counters |
|---|---|---|
| Gunship | Interceptors, AA Mechs, T2/T3 AA Turrets | Bases with insufficient anti-air |
| Machine-Gun Mech | Shock Towers, artillery, Mammoth Tanks; no anti-air | T1 turrets, T2 Machine-Gun Turrets, most low/mid-tier units |
| Heavy Tank / Plasma Tank | Artillery units, Experimental Tank | — |
| Low-HP masses (basic mechs, tanks, hover tanks) | Flamethrower Turret | — |
| Mechs in general | Shock (Shock Tower / T2 Shock Tower), artillery | — |
| Air (overall) | Interceptors, Heavy Interceptors, AA Mechs, Level-2/T3 AA Turrets, AA Fighters | Positions without anti-air |
| Enemy artillery units | Laser Defense Tower | Turrets / positions |
| Flying Fortress | Interceptors, AA Fighters | — |
| Battleship | (cannot hit air) needs anti-air cover | Low-tier positions, isolated Command Center (5+) |
| Self-Propelled Artillery | Dies easily without micro | — |
