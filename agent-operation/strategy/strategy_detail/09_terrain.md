## Mountainous Terrain

- On maps with **wide, intricate mountains**, **mech units** have a clear advantage.
- Mid/high-tier mechs such as Artillery Mechs and Machine-Gun Mechs can **ignore mountain obstruction**.

## Islands

If the enemy builds an outpost on an island, common ways to attack are:

1. **Air force**
2. **Navy** (when the island is small and within range)
3. **Find a way to transport ground troops**
4. **First win coastal sea control**: use ranged strikes (navy, artillery duels, T3 Machine-Gun Turret fire), then build a forward outpost under fire cover (naval Builders are usually a good choice) and produce troops on the spot

## Crossing Water

- Naval **anti-air** is usually a big problem.
- Fleets often **cannot fully destroy** an enemy base unless every key building is within reach of coastal ships.
- Use a ground hover-unit core: **Heavy Hovertank**, balanced in every respect, hits ground and air.

## Lava Zones

- Navy is completely unusable.
- But lava does not block **turret fire or artillery fire**.

## Terrain–Unit Cheat Sheet [Supplement]

| Terrain | Prefer | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| Mountains | Mechs (Artillery Mech, Machine-Gun Mech) | Mechs fear shock and artillery |
| Islands | Air, navy, transported ground; win sea control, then build | Naval anti-air is weak |
| Crossing water | Heavy Hovertank | Navy cannot fully destroy bases |
| Lava | Turrets, artillery | Navy is unusable |
