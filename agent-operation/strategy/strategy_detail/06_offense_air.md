# 06 Air Offense

[← Back to overview](../strategy.md) · Prev: [05 Land Offense](05_offense_land.md) · Next: [07 Naval Offense](07_offense_navy.md)

> The following are experience-based plays suited to human players, **for reference only**.

## Gunship (800)

- Built at a level-2 Air Factory; affordable, with **high fire rate, medium speed and low HP**.
- In numbers, it flattens any base with **insufficient anti-air** in a blink; human games often use it to **blitz enemy buildings**.
- Countered by Interceptors, Anti-Air Mechs and T2/T3 Anti-Air Turrets.

## Amphibious Jet

- **Shock damage and fairly high HP**.
- Can **dive** to attack surface units, sometimes used to **contest sea control**.

## Scout Drone

- No attack, but **thick HP, high speed and low price**.
- Besides **scouting**, it often **soaks anti-air damage at the front** during air assaults.

## Missile Airship & AA Fighter

- Lategame air aces.
- **Missile Airship**: heavy armor, used in mass to force through enemy defensive positions.
- **AA Fighter**: an absolute nightmare for enemy air.
- Used in bulk in well-funded games (40+/60+ is common).

## Flying Fortress

Relies on its high HP and ability to produce **basic production units** and **mid-tier ground mainstays**:

- **Use 1**: build outposts anywhere and produce mid-tier ground units on the spot.
- **Use 2**: preload ground troops, force deep into the enemy base, then **airdrop a huge force**.
- **Note**: it will be specifically targeted by Interceptors and AA Fighters.

## Using Air Power Well [Supplement]

- Scout first: when the enemy lacks anti-air, Gunships are the best value (see [03 Scouting](03_scouting.md)).
- Against Level-2 AA Turrets, Anti-Air Mechs or Interceptors, do not feed units in; use Scout Drones at the front to soak damage.
- Interceptors/Heavy Interceptors are the air counter, so beware of your own air force being singled out by enemy interceptors.
- When time is short, air can ignore enemy ground turrets (see [08 Attack Principles](08_tactics.md)).
