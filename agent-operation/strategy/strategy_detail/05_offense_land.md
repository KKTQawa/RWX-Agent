# 05 Land Offense

[← Back to overview](../strategy.md) · Prev: [04 Defense](04_defense.md) · Next: [06 Air Offense](06_offense_air.md)

> The following are experience-based plays suited to human players, **for reference only**.

## Heavy Tank + Plasma Tank

- Built at a level-2 Land Factory; affordable, and common in mass-army play.
- Fear: artillery units and artillery-type units such as the Experimental Tank.

## Machine-Gun Mech

A highly cost-effective high-tier unit: agile, **ignores cliffs**, fast fire rate, and often shines in human games.

- **Production**: needs a T2 Mech Factory (1000+5000); unit cost 4500+.
- **Strengths**: can snipe T1 turrets and T2 Machine-Gun Turrets, and bullies most low/mid-tier units.
- **Weaknesses**: cannot hit air (often escorted by 2+ Anti-Air Mechs); fears Shock Towers, artillery and Mammoth Tanks.

## Steel Torrent

A mass-army play built on **mid/high-tier ground mainstays**, including: Heavy Tank, Plasma Tank, Mammoth Tank, Machine-Gun Mech, Flamethrower Mech, and Anti-Air Mech / Heavy Anti-Air Mech / Anti-Air Missile Tank.

## Heavy Hovertank

Middling stats; a mid-tier hover mainstay. Slow, but still favored by human players in some situations (see crossing-water battles in [09 Terrain](09_terrain.md)).

## Ground Weakness Cheat Sheet [Supplement]

| Unit | Main weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Heavy Tank / Plasma Tank | Artillery units, Experimental Tank |
| Machine-Gun Mech | No anti-air; Shock Towers, artillery, Mammoth Tanks |
| Low-HP masses (basic mechs, tanks, hover tanks) | Flamethrower Turret |
| Mechs in general | Shock, artillery |

## Composition Tips [Supplement]

- Escort Machine-Gun Mechs with **2+ Anti-Air Mechs** to cover air.
- A Steel Torrent already includes anti-air units (AA Mech / Heavy AA Mech / AA Missile Tank); do not bring only ground-attack units.
- On mountainous maps, prefer mechs (see [09 Terrain](09_terrain.md)).
- Against many turrets or artillery, avoid plain Heavy Tank charges; first counter with Laser Defense and artillery (see [04 Defense](04_defense.md)).
