# 03 Scouting & Vision

## Scouting Tools

| Tool | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Spy Drone** | 1500 | Built at a T2 Air Base; high HP; made for scouting |
| **Watchtower** | T1 1500; T2 1500+2000 | A common vision extender. Usually placed **behind the front line** or where it covers **a large area of fog**. Note: **ordinary Builders cannot build it directly** |
| **Fast air units** | — | e.g. **Interceptors, Heavy Interceptors**, good for quick recon |
| **Scout Drone** | Low | No attack, but thick HP and high speed; besides scouting, often soaks anti-air fire at the front of an air assault (see [06 Air](06_offense_air.md)) |

> Resource point locations can be viewed through fog (see [01 Opening](01_opening.md)), so scouting is about **enemy composition and movement**, not finding resources.

## What to Scout [Supplement]

- **Enemy main units**: ground, air or naval? This decides how much to invest in anti-air, shock towers and artillery defense.
- **Ownership of outposts and resource points**: gauges the economy gap.
- **Attack routes**: mountains, islands and water limit unit types (see [09 Terrain](09_terrain.md)).
- **Before attacking**: check enemy anti-air and turrets (decides ground vs. air, see [08 Attack Principles](08_tactics.md)).

## Reminders [Supplement]

- Fast scouts arrive quickly and die quickly: do not park them inside enemy anti-air range.
- Place Watchtowers toward the rear: they see the front without being sniped first.
