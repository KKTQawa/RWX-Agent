# 04 Defense

[← Back to overview](../strategy.md) · Prev: [03 Scouting](03_scouting.md) · Next: [05 Land Offense](05_offense_land.md)

## Defensive Mindset

Artillery units (when funds allow) plus turrets at home are very cost-effective. **Do not underestimate turrets**: their defensive ceiling is close to the offensive ceiling of mobile units (unless the enemy is truly numerous).

## 1. Ground Turrets

| Turret | Price | Traits and use |
|---|---|---|
| **T2 Machine-Gun Turret** | — | The general-purpose home defense |
| **T3 Machine-Gun Turret** | 11000 | Wide range (second only to T2 Artillery), fast fire; one often gives strong defense, but expensive, **generally not used early** |
| **Flamethrower Turret** | 500+700 | Counters **numerous but low-HP** units such as basic mechs, tanks and hover tanks; slightly short range but high HP |
| **Shock Tower** | — | Good against **mechs**; built-in shield penetration, but slow fire rate |
| **T2 Shock Tower** | Expensive | Devastating against high-tier mechs |
| **Artillery Turret** | 1600 to upgrade | **Generally not used early**; its T2 range is the longest in the game, ideal for pushing the line in positional warfare |
| **T2 Artillery Turret** | Expensive | Longest range in the game; **generally not used early** |

## 2. Anti-Air

- **Level-2 Anti-Air Turret** (600+1200): the standard for position defense. **Level 1 gives only weak defense.**
- **Flak Cannon** (4000): slightly shorter range but strong area damage; a good option, though pricey.
- **T3 Anti-Air Turret** (12000): extremely strong anti-air with long range, but expensive.
- **Heavy Interceptor**: a strong answer to air, fast fire rate and speed.
- **Anti-Air Mech**: very cost-effective, area damage, moderate HP.

## 3. Repair Bay & Laser Defense

- **Repair Bay** (1500): if your army has **high-HP, high-tier units**, use it to repair wounded troops. **When money is tight, build at most one**; with a turret line, cover the turrets if possible.
- **Laser Defense Tower**: many uses, most commonly to defend against **enemy artillery**; upgrading to level 2 for 2000 is also common.

## 4. Artillery-Unit Defense

- **Heavy Artillery** (2000+) from a T2 Land Factory
- **Artillery Mech** (1700) from a level-1 Mech Factory

## 5. Choosing Defense by Threat [Supplement]

| Enemy threat | Recommended answer |
|---|---|
| Masses of low-HP units (basic mechs, tanks, hover tanks) | Flamethrower Turret |
| Mech-heavy (especially high-tier mechs) | Shock Tower / T2 Shock Tower |
| Air (Gunships, etc.) | Level-2 AA Turret; AA Mech; Heavy Interceptor; Flak Cannon (area damage) |
| Large lategame air | T3 AA Turret; Heavy Interceptors |
| Enemy artillery out-ranging you | Laser Defense Tower (upgradeable); your own artillery units |
| Need a solid general defense | T2 Machine-Gun Turret |
| Need to push the front in positional play | T2 Artillery Turret (expensive; later) |

## 6. Defensive Layout Tips [Supplement]

- After taking an outpost, build turrets **immediately**, then add anti-air (see [02 Holding Outposts](02_economy.md)).
- Cover turrets with the Repair Bay so the front-line turrets keep recovering.
- Do not let expensive T3 Machine-Gun or Artillery Turrets drag down your early economy; start with T2 Machine-Gun plus targeted turrets (Flamethrower/Shock Tower).
- Scale anti-air with enemy air strength: level 1 is not enough, so upgrade to 2; if still short, consider Flak Cannons or T3.
