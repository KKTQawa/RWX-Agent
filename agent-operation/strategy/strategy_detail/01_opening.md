# 01 Opening & Resource Points

[← Back to overview](../strategy.md) · Next: [02 Economy & Expansion](02_economy.md)

## Opening Action List

1. **Produce Builders**. This is routine no matter the situation.
2. **Scan all resource points on the map**. Resource point locations can be viewed through fog. Most maps have at least one resource point near your base.
3. **With no army, you can only do two things**: upgrade extractors and build turrets.
4. **Analyze the map's strategic locations** and decide where to expand. **Do not let most resource points fall into enemy hands**, or you will be at an overwhelming disadvantage.
5. **Confirm the economy multiplier and income tier** and let them guide what you build (see [02 Economy & Expansion](02_economy.md)).

## Why Resource Points Matter Most

Small and medium maps do not have many resource points. Knowing their distribution at the start and analyzing the strategic hubs is what lets you choose an expansion plan. The number of points you hold sets your later income tier, and once most are lost, micro rarely makes up the gap.

## How to Analyze Resource Points [Supplement]

- Sort points by ownership: **your nearby points**, **contested middle points**, **enemy nearby points**.
- Secure and upgrade your nearby points first, then contest the middle.
- Only take points you can hold: turret them immediately after capture (see [02 Holding Outposts](02_economy.md)).
- Watch the terrain too: islands, mountains and lava change who can reach and attack what (see [09 Terrain](09_terrain.md)).

## Opening Checklist [Supplement]

- [ ] Builders are in production
- [ ] Nearby extractors are being upgraded
- [ ] All resource points scanned and sorted
- [ ] Economy multiplier confirmed (x1 / x1.5 / x2+)
- [ ] First turret planned
- [ ] Opponent type/direction roughly judged; decide whether to scout early (see [03 Scouting](03_scouting.md))

## Common Opening Mistakes [Supplement]

- Building an army without looking at the resource point layout.
- Leaving money idle for long periods (see [10 Mechanics](10_realtime.md)).
- Early investment in expensive T3 Machine-Gun or Artillery Turrets (generally **not used early**, see [04 Defense](04_defense.md)).
