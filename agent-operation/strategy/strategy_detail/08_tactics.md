# 08 Tactics & Cautionary Units

[← Back to overview](../strategy.md) · Prev: [07 Naval Offense](07_offense_navy.md) · Next: [09 Terrain](09_terrain.md)

## 1. Mixed Units

- A very promising approach, and one of the strengths of traditional state-machine AI.
- Human players generally do not use mixed groups (not enough hands / single-threaded control).

## 2. Low-Tier Mass Rush

Usually effective only as an **early rush**. With more than 10 units you can run effective harassment and take an isolated Command Center.

| Unit | Price | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Basic mech | — | Mass rush in groups of 10+ |
| Tank | 350 each | Mass rush |
| Light Micro-Aircraft | 250 each | Built at a level-1 Air Base; usually used to snipe lone Builders in the field |
| Hover Tank | 450 each | Built at a level-1 Land Factory; usually used against light positions with only level-1 turrets |

## 3. Builder Play

- **Builder** (500): thanks to human agility, groups of Builders give **lightning-fast production** and can quickly **construct a Maginot turret line**.
- They can even build Laser Defense Towers and level-1 turrets on the spot to force-capture an isolated Command Center.

## 4. Cautionary Units

| Unit | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Helicopter** | Humans **almost never use it** |
| **Bomber** | Often used by the computer AI; humans use it only **in the lategame when rich**, to **bomb enemy Maginot positions** |
| **Shield Hovertank** (experimental, 21000) | Used only in a few well-funded games |
| **Tesla Mech** (T2 Mech Factory) | A shock unit, but **rarely used because it dies easily** |
| **Submarine** (800) | Rarely used by humans because it is not durable |
| **Self-Propelled Artillery** | Used a lot by humans, but dies easily, turns slowly and has low HP; **poor micro loses money** |

## 5. Attack Principles

- In a rush, **kill the buildings/units that can fight back first**.
- For ground armies, when time is short you can **ignore enemy anti-air turrets**.
- For air forces, when time is short you can **ignore enemy ground turrets**.

## 6. Pre-Attack Checklist [Supplement]

- [ ] Are economy and army at "Comfortable" or above, with outposts defended?
- [ ] Have you scouted the enemy's main units and anti-air/turret layout?
- [ ] Do your units counter theirs (see [11 Counters](11_counters_glossary.md))?
- [ ] Does the attack force cover both air and ground?
- [ ] Will units of different speeds arrive together (see [10 Real-Time Command](10_realtime.md))?
- [ ] Target priority: hit what can fight back first.

## 7. Common Mistakes [Supplement]

- Harassing with fewer than 10 rush units.
- Using Self-Propelled Artillery without micro, losing money.
- Sending Machine-Gun Mechs without anti-air escorts.
- Gambling with air against enemy air defenses and getting killed by Interceptors/Anti-Air Mechs.
