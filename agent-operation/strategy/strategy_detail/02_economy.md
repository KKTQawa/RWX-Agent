# 02 Economy & Expansion

[← Back to overview](../strategy.md) · Prev: [01 Opening](01_opening.md) · Next: [03 Scouting](03_scouting.md)

## 1. Watch the Economy Multiplier

Check the **economy multiplier** in the game settings (very important):

| Multiplier | Verdict |
|---|---|
| x1 | A very poor game |
| x1.5 | Barely workable |
| x2 and above | A normal, smooth game |

## 2. Read the Economy from Income

Income has two reporting bases:

- **In-UI native**: per 40 ticks, the number you feel in live play.
- **Per game second**: used for command output/evaluation; = native × 1.5625.

| Tier | Per 40 ticks (native) | ≈ Per game second | Advice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poor | under 50 | ≈78 | Land: only level-1 tanks or a few Heavy/Plasma Tanks (under ~1000 price). Mech factory: basic mechs/AA mechs with artillery mechs. Air: Interceptors/Heavy Interceptors and Gunships. Navy: Battleships |
| Normal start | 50–100 | ≈78–156 | Few limits; units priced up to ~2000 are fine |
| Comfortable | 100–200 | ≈156–312 | Strong attacks possible; consider nearly everything except a few very expensive units |
| Rich | 200+ | ≈312+ | Nukes can even be considered |

## 3. Read the Economy from Extractor Count

- In x2-and-above games, **stably holding 2–3 extractors** is enough to launch a small effective attack, provided they are **level 2 or higher, ideally level 3**.
- For safety, you can upgrade to the 8000 **Overclock mode** (Shield mode is poor value).

## 4. Hoard Economy with Resource Fabricators

- If there are no extractors left to capture, in x2+ games and a stable situation, use **Resource Fabricators** to raise income (the game has no economy cap).
- **Upgrade them to level 2 and 3 first** (bigger boost) instead of mass-building level 1.

## 5. Holding Outposts

- In most games, human players must defend outposts beyond the main base.
- After taking an outpost, **build turrets in the right place immediately**, then promptly hit long-range raiders and air units.

## 6. Rules for Spending Money

- Factories **deduct money the moment you order**; cancelling **refunds immediately**.
- A large idle balance has little benefit: unless saving for high-tier units/buildings, spend it promptly.

## 7. When to Switch to Offense [Supplement]

Consider attacking when most of these hold:

- Multiplier x2+, and you stably hold 2–3 extractors at level 2+ (ideally 3).
- Income has reached the "Comfortable" tier (100+ per 40 ticks).
- Your outposts have turrets and anti-air, so they will not be sniped.
- You have scouted the enemy's main units and picked counters (see [11 Counters](11_counters_glossary.md)).

## 8. Economic Decision Flow [Supplement]

```
Opening → take/upgrade extractors → judge multiplier and income tier
        │
        ├─ Poor (x1 / <50): cheap units + turret defense, hold resource points
        ├─ Normal: expand outposts; Resource Fabricators once stable (x2+)
        └─ Comfortable/Rich: build mid/high-tier units per tier; consider attacking or lategame tools
```
