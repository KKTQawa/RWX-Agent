# 10 Real-Time Command & Mechanics

[← Back to overview](../strategy.md) · Prev: [09 Terrain](09_terrain.md) · Next: [11 Counters, Prices & Glossary](11_counters_glossary.md)

## 1. Core Mechanics

- The game is typically about: **mass armies, Maginot turret lines, economy farming, and mass-nuke standoffs**.
- It is a **fast real-time game**: even 10 seconds of idling can overturn the situation.
- Factories **deduct money the moment you order**; cancelling **refunds immediately**.
- A large idle balance has little benefit: unless saving for high-tier units/buildings, spend it promptly.
- Interceptors are dedicated anti-air but need careful control; since they **can only attack air**, do not throw them away.
- Mechs generally fear shock and artillery, and most can ignore mountain obstruction.
- Shields usually regenerate on their own, but shock damage penetrates them.

## 2. Mind Movement Speed

- When you send an attack order, long distances let fast units reach the battlefield first, which can cause extra losses.
- Most units move at similar speeds, but **Interceptors, Heavy Interceptors, Transports and Scout Drones** are very fast.

**Suggestion [Supplement]**: for long attacks, gather units of different speeds at a staging point behind the front, then advance together; do not send fast units off at the same time as the main force.

## 3. Command Center

- Medium attack power; usually withstands light attacks.
- Built-in income of about **+18 per 40 ticks** (native UI) ≈ **+28 per game second** (at 1x income; the room's income multiplier applies on top).
- High HP and **can hit air**, but slow fire rate makes it easy to focus down.

## 4. Real-Time Play Tips [Supplement]

- **Keep tempo**: regularly check your balance and factory queues; do not let factories idle.
- **Manage Interceptors**: they only hit air, so give them targets and do not send them in alone.
- **Fragile units like Self-Propelled Artillery**: need micro; do not invest heavily without confidence.
- **Mind shock damage**: shields are penetrated by shock, so do not rely on shields against Shock Towers or shock units.
- **Keep checking the minimap and scouting info during attacks**; the situation can change in seconds.
