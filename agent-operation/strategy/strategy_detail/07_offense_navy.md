# 07 Naval Offense

[← Back to overview](../strategy.md) · Prev: [06 Air Offense](06_offense_air.md) · Next: [08 Tactics & Cautionary Units](08_tactics.md)

## Battleship (1500)

- The **best unit at the Naval Base, bar none**.
- **Cannot hit air**, but is very useful early and mid game **against low-tier enemy positions**.
- Its range is just above a T2 Machine-Gun Turret; 5+ can take an isolated Command Center.
- When money is tight, pair with **Interceptors** (anti-air) or **Missile Ships** (anti-air/submarine).

## Other Naval Notes

- **Submarine** (800): rarely used in human games because it is not durable.
- The **Amphibious Jet** can dive to attack surface units and sometimes contest sea control (see [06 Air](06_offense_air.md)).

## Naval Limitations

- Naval **anti-air** is usually a big problem.
- Fleets often **cannot fully destroy** an enemy base unless every key building is within reach of coastal ships.

## Navy in Different Terrain

- Islands: when the island is small and within range, navy is an option; you can also first win the coastal sea control and then build a base under fire cover (see [09 Terrain](09_terrain.md)).
- Crossing water: consider a ground hover-unit core (Heavy Hovertank).
- Lava: navy is unusable.

## Reminders [Supplement]

- Before sending ships, confirm the target is within coastal range, or you cannot deliver a decisive blow.
- Always bring anti-air (Interceptors or Missile Ships), or the fleet is easily punished by air.
